# PPTK 2026 — Design System & Direction

> **Face the Unknown**
> **Unlock Your Potential**

---

## 1. Project Identity

**PPTK 2026** is the digital archive and visual chronicle of the 2026 Computer Engineering student introduction program at Universitas Multimedia Nusantara.

The website is not primarily a functional portal or administrative website.

Its purpose is to preserve:

* People
* Activities
* Stories
* Achievements
* Photos
* Videos
* Memories
* The journey of PPTK 2026

The website should feel like a **digital time capsule**.

Years later, a student should be able to return to the website and recognize the people, moments, and experiences that shaped PPTK 2026.

---

# 2. Core Concept

## SYSTEM: UNKNOWN

PPTK 2026 is presented as a journey into the unknown.

The concept combines three visual ideas:

### Engineering

Represent the identity of Teknik Komputer through:

* Structured grids
* Technical labels
* Geometric shapes
* System-like typography
* Data-inspired elements
* Precise layouts

### Editorial

Inspired by the visual storytelling of PPIF, but NOT copying its visual design.

Use:

* Large typography
* Strong compositions
* Editorial layouts
* Photo cutouts
* Frames
* Stickers
* Arrows
* Layered imagery
* Asymmetric sections

### Human / Memory

The website must remain personal.

People and memories are more important than technical decoration.

Use:

* Real photographs
* Names
* Quotes
* Short stories
* Captions
* Personal moments
* Behind-the-scenes content

The final result should feel like:

> **An engineering-themed digital yearbook with editorial storytelling.**

---

# 3. Design Philosophy

The website should answer one question:

> "If someone from PPTK 2026 visits this website years later, will they feel like they are looking back at a chapter of their life?"

Therefore:

**Story > Decoration**

**People > UI**

**Photography > Generic Graphics**

**Experience > Information Density**

Technical elements should support the story, not overwhelm it.

---

# 4. Brand Identity

## Logo

Use the official PPTK logo as the primary identity.

Do not redraw, distort, rotate, or substantially modify the logo.

The logo may be used as:

* Navbar logo
* Hero element
* Section marker
* Footer mark
* Decorative geometric inspiration

The geometry of the logo may inspire background shapes, dividers, frames, and layout elements.

---

## Tagline

Primary tagline:

> **Face the Unknown**
> **Unlock Your Potential**

Use the tagline prominently in the hero.

It should feel like the central statement of PPTK 2026 rather than ordinary supporting copy.

---

# 5. Color System

The color palette should be derived from the PPTK logo.

### Primary Blue

`#557DCC`

Used for:

* Primary brand elements
* Large backgrounds
* Buttons
* Section accents
* Interactive states

### Primary Orange

`#E85A00`

Used for:

* Highlights
* Important labels
* Accent elements
* Calls to action
* Awards
* Timeline markers

### Dark Navy

`#101827`

Used for:

* Dark sections
* Footer
* High-contrast backgrounds
* Technical/system sections

### Off White

`#F7F5F0`

Used as the primary light background.

### White

`#FFFFFF`

Used for:

* Cards
* Text on dark sections
* Photography frames

### Color Rule

Do not use blue and orange equally everywhere.

Use:

* Blue as the dominant identity color
* Orange as a controlled accent
* Dark navy for contrast
* Off-white for breathing room

Avoid excessive gradients.

---

# 6. Typography

Use a modern geometric sans-serif for primary typography.

Recommended characteristics:

* Bold
* Modern
* Geometric
* Highly readable
* Strong at large sizes

Headings should be visually expressive.

Example:

> THE PEOPLE

> THE JOURNEY

> WE WERE HERE.

Supporting labels may use a monospace font to create a subtle engineering/system feeling.

Example:

`DIVISION // R&D`

`EVENT_03`

`PPTK_2026`

`ARCHIVE // 001`

Do not use monospace for long paragraphs.

---

# 7. Visual Language

The visual language should combine structured engineering elements with editorial compositions.

## Use

* Large typography
* Grid systems
* Geometric shapes
* Thin technical lines
* Small metadata labels
* Photo cutouts
* Image frames
* Slightly rotated photographs
* Arrows
* Stickers
* Layered elements
* Asymmetric compositions
* Large whitespace
* Strong contrast

## Avoid

* Generic SaaS dashboard design
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Excessive rounded cards
* Huge gradients
* Generic purple AI aesthetics
* Overly corporate layouts
* Excessive neon effects
* Excessive 3D elements
* Overly complex animations
* Copying PPIF's exact compositions

---

# 8. Overall Experience

The website should feel like a continuous story.

Recommended structure:

```text
HOME
│
├── THE BEGINNING
│
├── THE PEOPLE
│
├── THE JOURNEY
│
├── THE MOMENTS
│
├── THE HIGHLIGHTS
│
├── THE MEMORIES
│
└── THE LEGACY
```

These do not necessarily need to become separate routes.

A long-form homepage may contain most of the story, with dedicated pages used only when additional depth is necessary.

Prefer **one strong experience** over many shallow pages.

## Hero

The hero should immediately establish PPTK 2026.

Content:

**PPTK 2026**

**Face the Unknown**
**Unlock Your Potential**

Supporting text should be short.

Example:

> A story of the people, moments, and challenges that shaped PPTK 2026.

Possible CTA:

**EXPLORE THE JOURNEY**

The hero may use:

* Large photography
* Video
* Logo
* Geometric elements
* Subtle system-inspired animation

The hero should feel like the beginning of a journey.

---

# 10. THE BEGINNING

Introduce PPTK 2026.

Do not write a long institutional description.

Instead use a short narrative.

Example:

> Every generation starts somewhere.
>
> Before the projects, the late nights, the circuits, and the code, there was a first step.

Combine text with:

* One strong photograph
* Small metadata
* Date
* Short description

The section should establish emotional context.

---

Possible hierarchy:

```text
PPTK 2026
│
├── BPH
│
├── DIVISION
│   ├── Coordinator
│   └── Staff

└── OTHER ROLES
```

* Name

* Division
* Generation / cohort

`POSITION // STAFF`

`GENERATION // 2024`

Large portraits can be combined with:

* Technical labels
* Geometric frames
* Division markers
* Small metadata
* Subtle decorative elements

The people should remain the visual focus.

Each timeline entry may contain:

* Date

* Photo

# 18. Navigation

Keep navigation simple.

Recommended:

* Home
* People
* Journey
* Moments
* Highlights
* Memories

Navigation may remain sticky while scrolling.

Use section anchors when possible.

Avoid excessive menus.

---

# 19. Components

Core reusable components may include:

* Navbar
* Hero
* SectionHeader
* PersonCard
* DivisionSection
* TimelineItem
* GalleryItem
* GalleryLightbox
* AwardCard
* QuoteCard
* Stats
* Footer

Components should remain simple and reusable.

Do not create abstractions before they are actually needed.

---

# 20. Animation

Animation should support storytelling.

Recommended:

* Fade-in on scroll
* Slight slide/reveal transitions
* Image hover effects
* Timeline reveal
* Subtle parallax
* Smooth section transitions

Avoid:

* Excessive bouncing
* Constant motion
* Heavy 3D animations
* Long loading animations
* Animations that reduce readability

The website must remain performant.

---

# 21. Photography Direction

Photography is one of the most important assets.

Prioritize real PPTK photography.

Useful categories:

* Group photos
* Individual portraits
* Activities
* Preparation
* Behind the scenes
* Candid moments
* Team photos
* Final moments

Avoid relying heavily on stock photography.

Real people make the archive meaningful.

---

# 22. Content Architecture

Content should be separated from UI wherever practical.

Example:

```text
src/
├── data/
│   ├── people.js
│   ├── events.js
│   ├── awards.js
│   ├── memories.js
│   └── gallery.js
│
├── components/
├── pages/
├── assets/
└── styles/
```

The initial project may use static JavaScript data.

A backend/database is NOT required for the first version.

---

# 23. Technical Direction

Keep the technology simple.

Preferred:

* React
* Vite
* Vanilla CSS

Optional:

* React Router
* Intersection Observer
* Lightweight animation utilities only when necessary

Do NOT introduce:

* Large UI frameworks
* Complex state management
* Backend
* Database
* CMS

unless a real requirement appears later.

The goal is maintainability for a solo developer.

---

# 24. Responsive Design

The design must work on:

* Desktop
* Laptop
* Tablet
* Mobile

Desktop can use more experimental editorial compositions.

Mobile should prioritize:

* Reading order
* Image clarity
* Touch interaction
* Typography
* Performance

Do not simply shrink desktop layouts.

Recompose sections when necessary.

---

# 25. Complexity Constraint

PPTK should feel visually sophisticated without becoming technically excessive.

Target:

**Visual complexity: 7/10**

**Technical complexity: 5/10**

**Maintenance difficulty: 4/10**

PPIF may be used as a reference for visual ambition, but PPTK must remain substantially easier to develop and maintain.

---

# 26. Design Principles

Always prioritize:

1. Identity
2. Storytelling
3. People
4. Photography
5. Information
6. Interaction
7. Decoration

Do not reverse this order.

A beautiful animation is useless if it makes the story harder to understand.

---

# 27. What PPTK Is NOT

PPTK is NOT:

* A university administration portal
* A student dashboard
* A generic event landing page
* A SaaS-style website
* A corporate organization profile
* A copy of PPIF
* A technology showcase

PPTK IS:

> **A visual record of a generation's journey.**

---

# 28. Design North Star

Every design decision should support this sentence:

> **PPTK 2026 is a digital time capsule that documents the people, moments, challenges, and memories of a generation entering Teknik Komputer.**

If a feature does not support that purpose, question whether it needs to exist.
